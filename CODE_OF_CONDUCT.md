# Code of Conduct

SPAN follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text applies to everyone who takes part in this project: in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project is represented.

## In short

We want SPAN to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

**Do:**

- be kind, patient and respectful, especially to newcomers;
- give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, about the work rather than the person;
- assume good intent, and own your mistakes;
- focus on what is best for the project and its players.

**Don't:**

- harass, insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately;
- use sexualized language or imagery, or make unwelcome advances;
- troll, or make personal or political attacks;
- publish anyone's private information without their explicit permission;
- do anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [@AThraen](https://github.com/AThraen), privately through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful, following the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
